### Claim mapping

These are the three **TRUE, narrowly worded claims** approved after Phase 1. Evidence links are pinned to the reviewed baseline, `2f01522b1dea80e95e372057ff9a9d8c3cabec70`; this PR does not change the cited behavior or policy source.

| Page copy | Verdict | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recipes without a subscription.** “Read recipes here without signing up or paying for membership.” | **TRUE** | The former recipe paywall is [explicitly retired](https://github.com/zapcooking/frontend/blob/2f01522b1dea80e95e372057ff9a9d8c3cabec70/src/lib/consts.ts#L120-L124). Legacy premium URLs [redirect to ordinary recipes](https://github.com/zapcooking/frontend/blob/2f01522b1dea80e95e372057ff9a9d8c3cabec70/src/routes/premium/recipe/%5Bslug%5D/+server.ts#L3-L9), whose page [renders fetched recipe content without a membership gate](https://github.com/zapcooking/frontend/blob/2f01522b1dea80e95e372057ff9a9d8c3cabec70/src/routes/recipe/%5Bslug%5D/+page.svelte#L221-L224). |
| **Your recipes stay yours.** “Copy or print your recipes and keep them outside Zap Cooking.” | **TRUE** | [Terms §3](https://github.com/zapcooking/frontend/blob/2f01522b1dea80e95e372057ff9a9d8c3cabec70/src/routes/terms/+page.svelte#L58) retains users’ ownership while granting the service an operating license. Existing [copy-text and copy-JSON handlers](https://github.com/zapcooking/frontend/blob/2f01522b1dea80e95e372057ff9a9d8c3cabec70/src/components/Recipe/Recipe.svelte#L292-L306) and [printing](https://github.com/zapcooking/frontend/blob/2f01522b1dea80e95e372057ff9a9d8c3cabec70/src/components/Recipe/PrintRecipeModal.svelte#L213-L218) support keeping independent copies.
